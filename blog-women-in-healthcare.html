Reflection

A room full of women, finally off the clock

Janet Wanjiku · Women in Healthcare Dinner

The Women in Healthcare Dinner closed the day the way it should have, not with another panel, but with a room full of women finally off the clock.

There was dancing. There was networking that did not feel like networking, just women talking, actually listening, trading numbers because they meant to use them. Women doing genuinely great things, sitting across the table like it was nothing, when really it was everything.

There is a particular kind of energy in a room like that. Not the loud kind. The kind that shows up in how long people stayed past when they were supposed to leave, in how many conversations turned into real plans before the night was over, in the version of yourself that walks out a little more inspired than the one that walked in.

Sitting across the table like it was nothing, when really it was everything.

I left with sore feet from dancing, a fuller notebook, and that specific kind of tired that comes from being fully present rather than half checked out.

If you were in that room, you already know. If you were not, find your version of that dinner. It changes something.
