Change & transition

Life Transitions

Change, uncertainty, identity shifts and seasons of life that require a new direction. Support for finding solid ground while things are moving.

What this looks like

Transitions such as a new career, a relationship ending, becoming a parent, illness, relocation or loss can unsettle even the parts of life that felt stable. This work helps you slow down enough to understand what's actually shifting, grieve what needs grieving, and make decisions from clarity rather than panic.

Who it's for

Anyone in the middle of significant change, whether chosen or unchosen: a new chapter that hasn't found its shape yet, or an ending that hasn't fully been processed.

How sessions work

Available in person in Kiambu or online. Sessions are often shorter-term and focused, though some clients continue longer where the transition touches deeper, ongoing patterns.
