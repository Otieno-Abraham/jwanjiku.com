About Janet

Psychology that sees the whole person.

“A space to feel heard, understood and gently challenged toward your true north.”

Therapy can be a place to be honest about what is happening beneath the surface — without judgement, performance, or needing to arrive with all the answers.

Janet’s work is grounded in psychological understanding, thoughtful conversation and practical reflection. Her aim is to create a space where clients feel heard and seen while developing insight, self-awareness and healthier ways of moving through life.

Her experience includes individual, group and couples work across different levels of healthcare, supporting adolescents, young adults, women and high-functioning professionals through emotional, relational and life challenges.

The therapeutic relationship

Heard. Seen. Validated. Challenged.

Feeling understood matters. So does being willing to look honestly at the patterns, beliefs and choices that may be keeping you stuck. The aim is to offer both compassion and thoughtful challenge — supporting insight, responsibility and movement.

Heard

Your experience has room to be expressed.

Seen

We look beyond the immediate problem to the person underneath it.

Validated

Your feelings can make sense without every reaction needing to remain unchanged.

Challenged

Growth can require gentle honesty, new perspectives and intentional action.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

A few things clients often want to know before their first session.

Is everything I share confidential?+
Yes. Sessions are confidential in line with professional and ethical guidelines. The only exceptions are situations involving risk of serious harm, which will always be discussed with you directly wherever possible.
Do you offer online sessions?+
Yes. In-person sessions are available in Kiambu, and online sessions are offered for clients elsewhere in Kenya or abroad.
What happens in a first session?+
The first session is an opportunity to talk through what brings you to therapy, ask questions, and get a feel for how Janet works, so you can decide together on a suitable direction.
How long does therapy typically take?+
This varies by person and goals — some clients attend a short series of sessions around a specific issue, while others prefer ongoing, longer-term support. This is reviewed together as you go.
How do I book an appointment?+
Reach out by phone or email using the contact details below, and Janet will respond to arrange a suitable time.
