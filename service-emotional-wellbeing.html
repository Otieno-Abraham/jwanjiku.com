Self & emotional wellbeing

Self & Emotional Wellbeing

Self-esteem, anxiety, emotional regulation and personal development: space to understand yourself more clearly and build a steadier relationship with your own emotions.

What this looks like

This work centres on your internal world: how you relate to yourself, manage difficult emotions, and speak to yourself under pressure. For some clients that means addressing anxiety directly; for others it's about self-esteem, self-criticism, or simply feeling disconnected from their own needs. Sessions build both insight and practical tools you can use between appointments.

Who it's for

Anyone experiencing anxiety, low self-worth, emotional overwhelm, or a general sense of not feeling like themselves. This is often a starting point for clients who aren't yet sure what they need, and the space itself helps that become clearer.

How sessions work

Available in person in Kiambu or online. Early sessions usually focus on understanding what's happening and why; later sessions build the emotional skills and self-awareness that support lasting change.
